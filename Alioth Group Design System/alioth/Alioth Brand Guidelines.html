01 — IntroductionA peak, drawn twice

The mark is a single capital A, stripped to two nested strokes that rise to one point.

Alioth is the brightest star in the handle of the Big Dipper — historically the star navigators steered by. The brand takes that idea literally: a fixed point clients orient around.

The symbol reads as an A, but it is built like an apex — an outer ribbon that loops over at the top and two inner lines that climb toward the same summit. It is confident enough for a boardroom, expressive enough for a campaign, and refined enough to feel premium at any scale.

Black anchors the system · Cream gives it air · Deep Forest signals growth and momentum.

02 — The SymbolPrimary logo icon

The Alioth A

The icon is the heart of the identity and works on its own — as an app glyph, avatar, favicon, embroidery or signage cut. Three strokes, one summit:

  • 01 Outer ribbon — loops over at the apex, splays to two outer feet.
  • 02 Inner rise — a single line tucked beneath the crown.
  • 03 The mark — a clipped accent that completes the A.
